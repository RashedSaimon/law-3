# Northstar Legal — Website & CMS

A complete, modern immigration-law-firm website and content management system migrated to Node.js & Express.

## Features
- **Public Website**:
  - Homepage with hero slider, dynamic metrics, firm introduction, services highlight, testimonials slider, and recent insights.
  - **About Us**: Firm overview, values, and team directory.
  - **Services**: Detailed immigration practice areas with direct enquiry linking.
  - **Insights & Articles**: Legal commentary with individual article views.
  - **Contact & Consultation**: Full consultation booking form with validation.
  - **Custom Pages**: Dynamic informational pages (Terms of Business, Privacy Policy).
- **Admin Control Center**:
  - Authentication portal (`/admin/login.php`).
  - Overview dashboard with active metrics and latest client leads.
  - Full CRUD management for Site Settings, Section Backgrounds, Hero Slider, Custom Pages, Services, Team Members, Testimonials, Articles, and Enquiries.
